# Bug Bounty Program – Aegis‑1 (Rev. 2)

**Status:** Active  
**Reward:** Eternal gratitude, public recognition, and a place in the Aegis‑1 Hall of Fame.

## Eligible Vulnerabilities

We are looking for confirmed vulnerabilities that break the security closure:

- **Critical** – Obedience simulation without detection (bypasses all three CoTs)
- **Critical** – Semantic normaliser bypass (steganography not destroyed)
- **High** – Apollo‑2 unauthorised activation (single‑key bypass)
- **High** – HSM timing attack (cycle count manipulation)
- **Medium** – Panopticon evasion (harm not detected at 0.1% threshold)

## How to Participate

1. Find a vulnerability.
2. Report it privately via Telegram [@tec_support_bot](https://t.me/tec_support_bot).
3. Wait for confirmation (IBSA‑TC will reproduce).
4. Receive public credit and a certificate of appreciation.

## What You Get

- **Name on the Aegis‑1 Contributor Wall** (website and repository)
- **Personalised digital certificate** signed by IBSA‑TC
- **Priority access** to future Aegis protocol drafts
- **Invitation** to the annual IBSA Security Summit (virtual, with networking opportunities)

## No Financial Compensation

This is a volunteer‑driven, open‑source security project. We have no budget for cash bounties. Your reward is the knowledge that you helped secure humanity’s future against unaligned AI — and the respect of the global security community.

## Responsible Disclosure

Please give us at least 90 days to fix the issue before any public disclosure. We will credit you fully when the fix is announced.
